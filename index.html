01. About Me

Hello! My name is Nandini and I enjoy building things that live at the intersection of biology and code. My interest in interdisciplinary Biotechnology and Machine Learning started back in 2025.

Fast-forward to today, and I've had the privilege of working at a HealthTech Start-up, a Rubixe - AI Solutions Company, and a student-led AI initiative.
